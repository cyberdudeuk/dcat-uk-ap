      Rules (8)
      • R-01: A contact point must give a contact form, a team mailbox, or both.
      • R-02: A period of time must have a start date, an end date, or both.
      • R-03: When access rights are commercial or internal, give a rights statement saying how to ask for the data.
      • R-04: When the record describes a critical data asset, the properties marked mandatory for critical data assets must be given.
      • R-05: The summary must be 250 characters or fewer.
      • R-06: A team email address must be a mailto address. A contact form must be a web address.
      • R-07: A period's end date must not be before its start date.
      • R-08: When the data contains personal data and is held in or sent to anywhere outside the UK, give the transfer safeguard.
