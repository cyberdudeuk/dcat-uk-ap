    Value types

    Updated 1 section on this page is new or updated since 1.2.0-rc.1 (2026-11-06). Everything that changed

    Every property takes one kind of value. These are the 13 kinds DCAT-AP-UK uses, how each is written in RDF and in JSON, and which properties use it. <abbr class="prov K" title="Background knowledge, not yet checked at the source" data-tip="Background knowledge, not yet checked at the source">K</abbr>
