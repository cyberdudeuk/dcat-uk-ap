    Check records

    Updated 1 section on this page is new or updated since 1.2.0-rc.1 (2026-11-06). Everything that changed

    Paste one record, or a list of records, as JSON, or as YAML in the xGov MEM form. The check runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

    Your recordsUpdated

    Adds the properties and rules of a sector. About extensions

    JSON, or simple YAML for xGov MEM records.

    How the check works

    It applies every obligation, cardinality, codelist and rule in the profile, using the same profile file the SHACL shapes are generated from. Converting an xGov MEM record uses the crosswalk, and lists what it changed.
