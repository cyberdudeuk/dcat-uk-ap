    The profile

    The profile at a glance

    Updated 2 sections on this page are new or updated since 1.2.0-rc.1 (2026-11-06). Everything that changed

    DCAT-AP-UK has 14 classes and 89 properties. Each class lists the properties a record of that kind can have, and whether each is mandatory, recommended, optional or conditional.

    Dataset

    A collection of data, published or curated by one organisation, available in one or more forms.

    48 properties, 9 mandatory

    34 properties, 10 mandatory

    Distribution

    Recommended dcat:Distribution

    A specific form of a dataset, such as a CSV file, a GeoPackage or an API response.

    18 properties, 1 mandatory

    What the obligations mean

    Mandatory
    Every record must have it.
    Recommended
    Give it whenever you can. Harvesters and search rely on it.
    Optional
    Give it when it helps the people who will use the data.
    Conditional
    Mandatory when the stated condition applies.

    A record conforms when it meets every mandatory and conditional rule. When the record describes a critical data asset, more properties become mandatory: they are marked "M for critical data assets".

    Rules

    Rules that span more than one property
    RuleApplies toWhat it says
    R-01Contact pointA contact point must give a contact form, a team mailbox, or both.
    R-02Period of timeA period of time must have a start date, an end date, or both.
    R-03DatasetWhen access rights are commercial or internal, give a rights statement saying how to ask for the data.
    R-04DatasetWhen the record describes a critical data asset, the properties marked mandatory for critical data assets must be given.
    R-05DatasetThe summary must be 250 characters or fewer.
    R-06Contact pointA team email address must be a mailto address. A contact form must be a web address.
    R-07Period of timeA period's end date must not be before its start date.
    R-08DatasetWhen the data contains personal data and is held in or sent to anywhere outside the UK, give the transfer safeguard.
