    Tools

    Check many records

    Updated 1 section on this page is new or updated since 1.2.0-rc.1 (2026-11-06). Everything that changed

    Fill in the spreadsheet template, one row per dataset, or export your catalogue as CSV. Load it here to convert every row to DCAT-AP-UK, check it, see its conformance level and quality score, and download the records. Nothing leaves your browser.

    Download the template (CSV)

    Your recordsUpdated

    Saved from a spreadsheet as CSV (comma separated), with the column names in the first row.

    Adds the properties and rules of a sector. About extensions

    The columns

    Each column is a property name, as in the data dictionary. A part of a property is named with a dot, such as contactPoint.hasURL or distribution.mediaType. Separate repeated values with a vertical bar and spaces, as in postcode | address. Leave a cell empty when you have nothing to say. One distribution per row; add more in the record builder or your catalogue.

    All columns in the template

    id, title, description, abstract, identifier, publisher, contactPoint.fn, contactPoint.hasURL, accessRights, rights, securityClassification, personalData, licence, accrualPeriodicity, criticalDataAsset, creator, created, issued, modified, theme, keyword, spatial, temporal.startDate, temporal.endDate, conformsTo, landingPage, language, jurisdiction, transferMechanism, distribution.title, distribution.accessURL, distribution.downloadURL, distribution.mediaType, distribution.byteSize
